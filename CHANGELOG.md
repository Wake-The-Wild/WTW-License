# Changelog

All notable changes to the Wake The Wild License are documented here.

## 1.0 — 2026-09-28

- Initial public version.
- Permits personal gameplay and use of unmodified official releases on private
  and public servers, subject to the stated conditions.
- Permits private and public modpacks containing an unmodified official release
  without requiring prior permission, subject to attribution, distribution,
  content, and monetization conditions.
- Defines rules for independently authored add-ons, integrations, gameplay
  media, branding, monetization, archived projects, and separate permissions.
- Reserves WTW code, assets, branding, and text-and-data-mining rights.
- Establishes general legal terms, termination rules, warranty disclaimers,
  and versioning behavior.
