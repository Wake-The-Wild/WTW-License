# Community maintenance request

Archiving or discontinuing a WTW project does not automatically permit a fork,
modified release, or community continuation. Send this completed form to
**wakethewild.contact@gmail.com**.

```text
Subject: WTW community maintenance request — [PROJECT NAME]

Requester name or organization:
Contact email:
Relevant developer profiles and previous projects:

WTW project and version:
Why is community maintenance being requested?
Proposed scope of maintenance:
Proposed repository and distribution platforms:
Will the work be commercial or monetized? If yes, describe how:
Requested use of WTW code, assets, project name, and branding:
Planned security, review, release, and support process:
Other contributors who would receive access:
Any other relevant information:
```

Submitting this request does not grant permission. Only an explicit written
Community Maintenance Notice issued by WTW grants rights, and that notice may
limit the project, versions, recipients, duration, branding, distribution, and
other conditions.
